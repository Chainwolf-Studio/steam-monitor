First, a big thank you to everyone who has tried One Hit Hero and shared their thoughts with us. We’ve been really happy to see people playing and to hear what you think.
We’ve had two rounds of playtesting so far, starting at Games of Transylvania, where you gave us a hefty list of bugs, suggestions, and feature requests. Ahead of the demo’s November 2 release, we focused on fixing as many bugs as we could. That meant the feature requests had to wait a little longer.
Across both rounds, some frustrations stood out, especially around boss collisions and keeping the action in view. In a game where any hit is fatal, a death that feels unfair is particularly frustrating. This first batch of changes tackles those problems and starts putting more of your suggestions into the game.
Here’s the full changelog:
Combat & fairness
No more standing inside bosses.
You can no longer walk or run into the Tutorial Cube, the Dice, or the Rock Sphere (including its twins). If a boss rolls or lands on top of you, you’re gently eased out of the way instead of getting stuck inside it and losing sight of the fight.
Dodge through attacks, not idle bosses.
Your dodge roll can still carry you through a boss while it’s attacking, but not while it’s standing idle.
Blocking the Tutorial Cube’s roll
now pushes you back further, keeping you in front of the cube instead of letting it roll into you.
Blocking the Rock Sphere’s Ricochet
now makes the sphere glance off your shield to your left while you’re knocked back in the opposite direction. Its next bounce always heads off to the side instead of potentially coming back at you from behind (happened a lot when the player was hugging the outer edge of the arena).
Camera & lock-on
Switch targets while locked on.
Flick the mouse sideways or push the right stick to switch between bosses, which is especially handy against the twin spheres.
While locked on, you can now 
nudge
the camera with the mouse or right stick to adjust your view.
New accessibility option: “Face boss instead of locking on”.
With this enabled, the lock-on button quickly turns the camera toward the boss, then leaves it free. The tutorial adapts its camera lesson to match.
Lock-on 
no longer drops
when a parry briefly freezes the boss.
After you respawn in a boss arena, 
the camera now faces the fight
instead of a random direction.
Tutorial
The movement lesson no longer asks you to look around first. Just get moving.
The tutorial totem can no longer be activated before you’ve finished the movement lessons.
Retrying the tutorial fight now points your camera at the Tutorial Cube.
Menus
Fixed the mouse cursor staying hidden after choosing 
Quit to Main Menu
.
Fixed a typo in the credits.
If you ran into any of these issues, we’d love to hear how the changes feel when you give the demo another go. Please keep sharing your feedback and bug reports here in the Community Hub.
Thank you for helping us make One Hit Hero better. It means a lot to both of us!
