## 15. Too Angry to Clean

- **AppID:** 5390660
- **Title:** What combat looks like in Too Angry to Clean
- **Published:** 2026-11-13T10:51:02Z
- **Author:** (not provided)
- **URL:** https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/5390660/view/744943222106620333
- **GID:** 744943222106620333

### Announcement text

The game has changed a lot over the past few months. It used to be all about destruction, and now there are undead, waves and bosses, so it needed real combat. Here's what's in it right now. 
Magic
There will be nine spells, and eight are already done: fireball, ice grenade, magnetism, telekinesis, lightning, repulsion, triple phantom strike and the nuke. I'm still thinking about the ninth.

Each spell has its own cast animation and its own effect, plus its own stats and its own way of affecting the surroundings. Telekinesis, for example, lets you yank an enemy out of the crowd and hurl it right back in. You can do the same with any object: a house, or your friend in co-op. Lightning is cast from both hands in turn and hits instantly, while the nuke has a huge blast radius but costs a lot to cast. 
Mana and dash
Every spell now costs mana. You don't have much at first, but regen grows as your character develops, and so does your mana pool. For dodging I made a dash that works both on the ground and in the air. It eats mana too, but you can cut that cost with upgrades. The heroine is invulnerable during the dodge, like in Dark Souls. 
Active skills
There are ten of them at the moment. A couple of examples. "Centrifuge": you take off, spin up and steer your flight, smashing everything and everyone in your path. "Meteor": you drop from above at huge speed and wreck everything where you land. "Boiling Point": for a few seconds all your stats go up, flight is unlimited, and you can tear through everything, roughly like Kratos. "Storm": the heroine rises into the air and lightning strikes in every direction. "Nap Time": a circle appears on the ground; inside it a downed ally gets back up instantly (in co-op), health and mana recover fast, you take less damage, and enemies get bogged down. 
Synergies
Neighbouring branches are linked. Say you've upgraded "Chaos" and "Storm". "Chaos" drives nearby enemies mad and they start fighting each other. If the switch is set to "Storm", everyone it hit who survived falls into chaos for 5 seconds. If it's set to "Chaos", anyone left under 20% health after it gets finished off by lightning. 
Ground and air
You can fly freely, and there's a separate branch for aerial combat. From above your damage is usually higher and you're more agile, but you can't just hide up there. Skeletons throw bones, mages fire spells, and the first boss (almost done, it'll be in the playtest) can jump up to you at any height, and very fast.

In the end you can build a ground tank, an aerial assassin, a support and so on, and mix branches however you like. In co-op this works especially well, when everyone on the team plays their own way. But there's plenty of room in solo too, and you'll be able to level up almost everything. The main goal I set for myself is to make combat fast and interesting. As a fan of Soulslikes and God of War, I'd never forgive myself if the combat turned out boring.

In one of the next posts I'll write about how the game's economy works, how you earn the in-game currency (by the way, I haven't come up with a name for it yet either) and about difficulty levels. After that we'll talk about the story, which I'm also actively working on right now. It'll be simple, with a couple of interesting twists. But that's still better than no story at all. I'm no Miyazaki, after all, to write great stories on my own.

Oh, and one more thing before I forget: an answer to a frequent question. Why is the game called that? Honestly, it's just a joke about all the cleaning games that have flooded Steam. The original idea was that instead of cleaning up, you cause total chaos, and I think that worked out pretty well.

Thanks everyone for reading!
